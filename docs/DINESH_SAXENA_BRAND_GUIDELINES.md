# Dinesh Saxena Official Brand Guidelines

## 1. Purpose and authority

This document is the working specification for every Dinesh Saxena brand expression: website, social profiles, presentations, training collateral, proposals, certificates of participation, event materials, and future downloadable assets.

The brand presents Dinesh Saxena as a distinguished hospitality professional, food-safety specialist, trainer, and independent consultant/mentor. Its promise is to translate lived hospitality experience and standards into practical human behaviour, stronger capability, and better operational outcomes.

When artwork, copy, or contact details conflict with this document, pause publication and obtain owner confirmation. Never infer credentials, relationships, social handles, domains, client names, or achievements.

## 2. Brand character

The identity should feel:

- Heritage-led, not nostalgic.
- Executive and credible, not corporate-generic.
- Warm and human, not informal.
- Disciplined and operational, not bureaucratic.
- Wise and reflective, not self-important.
- Curious, resilient, and committed to lifelong learning.

The narrative arc is: IHM Dadar → Taj ecosystem → food-and-beverage operations → training and learning & development → Taj SATS/airline catering → independent training and consulting.

## 3. Core logo concept

### Primary brand mark

The immutable core logo is a sophisticated single-letter **D** monogram or crest. The letterform should suggest hospitality heritage, leadership, wisdom, journey, learning, and resilience without becoming an illustrative collage.

Permitted supporting ideas, used sparingly:

- An open book or page edge for learning.
- A path or subtle rising line for professional journey.
- A restrained sunrise for optimism and renewal.
- Architectural details associated with gracious hospitality.
- A freshly brewed coffee cup as a cue for warmth, reflection, and conversation.

The mark must remain legible at small sizes. Fine details should disappear gracefully rather than becoming visual noise.

### Portrait treatment

An illustrated or refined cartoon-infographic silhouette may accompany the monogram in secondary expressions. It should be inspired by the approved Dinesh Saxena portrait and retain these cues:

- Recognisable glasses.
- Camel/tan blazer.
- Dark patterned shirt.
- Confident, composed mentor posture.
- Warm, approachable facial treatment without caricature.

The portrait is a supporting brand element, not a replacement for the primary D monogram.

### Coffee-only rule

When a beverage motif is used, it must be a freshly brewed coffee cup. Never use beer, wine, spirits, cocktail glasses, liquor bottles, or any alcohol imagery.

### Taj inspiration and non-affiliation

Hospitality heritage may inform proportion, restraint, service cues, architectural detail, and atmosphere. Never copy the Taj logo, wordmark, typography, symbols, proprietary visual identity, or trade dress. Never imply current Taj affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement, or official partnership. Historical experience may be described factually and accurately.

## 4. Logo system and lockups

### Primary lockup

The primary lockup contains the core D monogram and the name **Dinesh Saxena**. It is the default for the website header, presentation title pages, proposals, stationery, and high-visibility brand use.

### Secondary lockup

The secondary lockup may add the approved professional descriptor beneath or beside the name:

> Hospitality Professional · Food Safety Specialist · Trainer & Independent Consultant

Use this where the audience needs immediate role context. Do not compress this long descriptor into spaces where it becomes illegible; use the primary mark alone instead.

### Digital signature / contact lockup

The digital signature lockup may combine the approved logo with a verified website URL and verified social handles. It is intended for website footers, social profile artwork, presentation end slides, email signatures, and selected collateral.

The digital signature is not the core logo. Social handles, URLs, QR codes, phone numbers, and other mutable details must never be embedded in the immutable primary mark. This separation prevents the official logo from becoming obsolete when contact information changes.

### Logo exclusions

Do not:

- Add certification claims, current employer marks, social handles, URLs, QR codes, phone numbers, or taglines inside the core mark.
- Stretch, skew, rotate, outline, bevel, or add drop shadows to the logo.
- Recolour individual logo details outside the approved palette.
- Place the mark over noisy imagery or insufficient contrast.
- combine the mark with a third-party logo without documented approval.

## 5. Clear space and minimum size

Let **x** equal the interior stroke width or counter width of the final D monogram, whichever is larger.

- Maintain at least **1x** clear space on every side of a standalone mark.
- Maintain at least **1.5x** around a full name lockup.
- No text, rule, photo edge, icon, or container boundary may enter this space.
- Minimum digital height: **24 px** for the standalone D monogram.
- Minimum digital height: **36 px** for the primary name lockup.
- Minimum print height: **8 mm** for the standalone mark and **12 mm** for the primary lockup.

If the detailed portrait or coffee motif becomes indistinct at the required size, use the simplified D monogram.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Heritage | Muted Gold | `#D4AF37` | Restrained highlights, fine rules, selected mark details |
| Warmth | Warm Ivory | `#F8F9FA` | Quiet backgrounds and breathing space |
| Clarity | White | `#FFFFFF` | High-contrast negative space and reversed applications |

### Supporting colours

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Food safety | Emerald | `#137547` | Food-safety cues, positive actions, small accents |
| Body text | Charcoal | `#212529` | Long-form copy and neutral information |

Navy should establish authority, ivory/white should preserve openness, and gold should remain selective. Emerald is an accent for food-safety context and calls to action; it must not overtake the identity. Avoid one-colour layouts and ornamental gradients that dilute the executive character.

### Approved logo colourways

1. Executive Navy on Warm Ivory or White.
2. Executive Navy with restrained Muted Gold detail on Warm Ivory or White.
3. Reversed White/Warm Ivory with Muted Gold detail on Executive Navy.
4. Single-colour black or white only when reproduction constraints require it.

## 7. Typography

Use **Plus Jakarta Sans** as the preferred digital family. **Inter** is an acceptable operational fallback. Use strong weights for headings, regular or medium weights for body copy, and generous line height for readability.

An elegant serif may appear sparingly in philosophical statements, reflective quotations, or premium editorial moments. It must never replace the main sans-serif system or create an ornamental luxury aesthetic.

Guidelines:

- Headlines: concise, calm, and confident; sentence case preferred.
- Body text: practical, direct, and easy to scan.
- Labels: short uppercase treatments are acceptable with restrained spacing.
- Avoid condensed novelty fonts, script fonts, and exaggerated display faces.
- Never distort letterforms to fit a container.

## 8. Imagery and illustration

Use real, inspectable imagery of Dinesh, training environments, hospitality operations, food-safety practice, learning, and mentorship. Favour natural, credible scenes over generic stock photography.

Illustration should use navy linework, muted gold details, warm ivory space, camel/tan clothing cues, and optional restrained emerald. Books, a path, sunrise, or architectural hospitality forms should support the story, not compete with the person.

Never use alcohol imagery. Never present stock participants as named clients or imply an engagement that has not been verified.

## 9. Messaging architecture

### Core promise

Translate lived hospitality experience and standards into practical human behaviour, capability, and better outcomes.

### Approved candidate lines

- Better People. Brighter Tomorrows.
- Life’s Learnings. A Better Tomorrow.
- Train. Inspire. Transform.

Choose one line per application; do not stack all three. Treat them as candidate messaging until an owner selects a permanent primary line.

### Values

People · Service · Hospitality · Leadership · Lifelong Learning

### Trainer’s philosophy

> “Effective food safety is not created by rules alone. It is created when standards become part of everyday operational behaviour.”

### Voice

Write in a calm, seasoned, precise, reflective, practical, and human voice. Prefer concrete operational language over promotional superlatives. Avoid influencer language, fabricated authority, generic motivational clichés, guaranteed results, and unsupported claims.

## 10. Digital integration

The website is the digital headquarters and source of truth. Every public surface must read identity and contact values from the central brand configuration rather than duplicating them.

Asset responsibilities:

- `primaryLogo`: immutable D-monogram artwork.
- `secondaryLockup`: logo with name and approved descriptor.
- `digitalSignatureLockup`: logo plus verified mutable contact/social information.
- `profilePortrait`: owner-approved portrait, maintained separately from the logo.
- `favicon`: simplified official mark designed for very small sizes.
- `openGraphImage`: purpose-built social preview with safe margins and minimal text.

The official logo should appear consistently in the navigation, profile treatment where appropriate, footer, favicon/app icon, social preview, and future downloadable brand assets after the approved files are uploaded.

### Social and URL verification

Only render a website link, social icon, handle, QR code, canonical URL, `sameAs` value, or social metadata when its destination has been verified by the owner. A visually plausible profile is not sufficient evidence.

## 12. Governance and handoff checklist

Before publishing a new brand asset or digital update:

1. Confirm the application uses the latest approved primary logo artwork.
2. Confirm mutable contact details are outside the core mark.
3. Confirm every visible URL, handle, and QR destination is owner-verified.
4. Confirm no Taj branding is copied and no current affiliation is implied.
5. Confirm all beverage imagery is freshly brewed coffee only.
6. Confirm clear space, minimum size, contrast, and colour rules are respected.
7. Confirm claims, engagements, testimonials, and partnerships have evidence.
8. Confirm the voice remains calm, practical, and free of exaggeration.
9. Update the central brand configuration first; do not scatter values across files.
10. Record the owner’s approval date and archive the approved source artwork.

Any future logo, website, social, presentation, or collateral work must preserve this visual vocabulary and positioning. A new palette or unrelated metaphor requires an explicit brand review.
